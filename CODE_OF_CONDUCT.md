# Code of conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version
2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. The
full text at that link applies to everyone who takes part here: in issues, pull requests,
reviews, discussions and any other project space, and when representing the project elsewhere.

## In short

- Be welcoming, patient and kind, whatever someone's background, identity or experience.
- Disagree about code and ideas, not about people. Give and accept constructive criticism.
- Do not harass, insult, demean or threaten anyone, publicly or privately. No sexualised language
  or imagery, no personal or political attacks, no trolling.
- Do not publish anyone's private information (an address, an email, a document they shared with
  you) without their explicit permission.
- Take responsibility when you get it wrong, apologise to those affected, and learn from it.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behaviour that breaks this code, report it privately to
`support@ubunex.com` with "Conduct: ubu-office-engine" in the subject. This is the same contact as
in `SECURITY.md`. Reports are read by the project maintainers only and handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that
break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone from the project's spaces, following
the enforcement guidelines (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban) in the Contributor
Covenant.
